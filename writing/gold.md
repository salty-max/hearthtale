- I counted my coin that evening and found a gold piece among it, the first I had ever had to my name.
- My purse held a whole gold piece for the first time, and I caught myself checking that it was still there.
- For the first time I owned a gold piece. It weighed almost nothing, and felt like a great deal.
- A gold piece of my own, at last: the coppers and the silver had added up, slowly, to something.
- I had my first gold piece, and began, a little dangerously, to think about what it could buy.
- The first gold piece came to my purse almost without my noticing, and after that I could not stop noticing it.
