- [!night] Thousand Needles was well named: stone pillars rose from the canyon floor by the hundred, with rope bridges strung between their tops. To the east, the Shimmering Flats shone white.
- [night] At night the needles of the canyon stood like a forest of black stone. The camps on the mesas showed small lights, and the drop below them was a long one.
