- [!night] The Badlands were dust and red rock under a sun with no mercy in it. The bones of great dragons lay half buried in the canyons, and nothing grew that did not have thorns.
- [night] The Badlands cooled quickly after dark, the canyons black and the stars hard and close. Somewhere a coyote called, and the echo came back from three directions at once.
