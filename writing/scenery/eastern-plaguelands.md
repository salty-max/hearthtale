- [!night] The Eastern Plaguelands were a land without colour, the trees grey, the rivers foul and the ground soft with plague. Stratholme's walls stood to the north, and nothing living went there willingly.
- [night] The Eastern Plaguelands were darker than any night should be. Things walked among the dead trees, and the only kind light came from the chapel at Light's Hope, small against the dark.
