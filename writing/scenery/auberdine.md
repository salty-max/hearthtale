- [!foe] Grey sea mist hung over the piers of Auberdine, and the boats came and went through it without hurry. I could see how a wait on this shore might stretch into days.
- [foe] The ships moored at Auberdine told me plainly whose shore this was. I admired the shelter of the piers, and did not expect a welcome beneath them.
