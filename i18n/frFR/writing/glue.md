# French: the pieces Diary.lua joins

Each key, then its French under "  > ". {text} is a sentence already told in
French (its first letter lowered where it follows), {lead} a place with its
preposition ("dans la vallée des Frigères"), {mates} the company, {things}
what my hands made, {at} a place with its preposition.

- with
  > Avec {mates}, {text}
- with-lead
  > {lead}, avec {mates}, {text}
- lead
  > {lead}, {text}
- i
  > Je {text}.
- busy
  > ai aussi fabriqué {things} de mes mains
- title-death
  > Mort {at}
- title-deaths
  > Morts {at}
