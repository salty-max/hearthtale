# Hearthtale in French: the lines

Each English line of `writing/` (its text exactly, tags left out), then its
French under `>`. The writer chooses its lines in English, then tells the
chosen one in French: a line with no French here leaves its whole entry in
English. Gender: `$gmasc:fem;` agrees with the narrator. Places, foes and
deeds come in by their slots: `{at}` "dans la vallée des Frigères", "à
Courbenclume"; `{foe}` "un jeune troll Frostmane"; `{why}` the deed after
"je" ("ai tué Grik'nir le Froid ..."). Names as Forever writes them; Classic's
are swapped in on Classic. The tense of a diary: passé composé and imparfait.
