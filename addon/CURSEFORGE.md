# Wayfarer's Journal

<!-- Project description for curseforge.com (paste as the project's description). -->

**Your character's own journal, written as you play.** Every level is a chapter, in your character's own voice: where the road went, the work you took and who asked it of you, what you fought and what nearly killed you, who walked with you, what you learned, what you found. On Hardcore, a death closes the book with an epitaph, and the life joins the Hall of the Fallen.

For Classic Era (Hardcore, Season of Discovery), TBC Anniversary and World of Warcraft: Forever. A sibling of [Lorekeeper's Codex](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/lorekeepers-codex) and [Explorer's Field Journal](https://github.com/salty-max/field-journal), in the same look, but it stands alone.

## A page of it

> I could not sleep in Kharanos, so I walked. Rejold Barleybrew kept me busy: "The Perfect Stout" and "Protecting the Herd". I lost count of the Winter Wolves in Iceflow Lake; my tally says fifteen. I'd heard the name Timber at every campfire. There I ended it.

## What a chapter tells

- **The road**: where and when the level began, the lands and places seen for the first time, the inn you made your home, your flights.
- **The work**: the quests you turned in, by name, and who gave them.
- **The fights**: what you fought most and where, the first of each kind of creature, elites, rares, and the close calls (under a tenth of your health, and alive to tell it), by night or day.
- **The company**: who you grouped with, the dungeons you went into and the bosses who stayed there.
- **The rest**: what the trainer taught you, your professions' milestones, the best thing you found, the time it took and the gold it brought.

A character you already play gets a prologue from what the game knows of its life so far, and chapters from there.

## How it is written

- In the first person, with the turns of phrase of your race and class: a dwarf's beard and ale, a Forsaken's second life, a paladin's Light, a hunter's pet.
- Graver on Hardcore, and graver still as the levels climb.
- Some 500 sentences, chosen to fit the moment: the hour, the first time or the tenth, how close the call, alone or in a group. A place just named becomes "there"; a sentence never comes back within a few chapters.
- Written from what the addon records each time you open the book: nothing but the records is saved, and better sentences in later versions reach your old chapters too.

## Hardcore: the Hall of the Fallen

When a Hardcore character dies, its book closes: an epitaph telling how it ended and what the life was, a line in chat and the game's alert. The book joins the **Hall of the Fallen**, shared by all your characters, where every fallen life can be read again, chapter by chapter. On other realms, a death is told in its chapter, and the book goes on.

## The book

`/wayfarer` (or `/wj`), or the book by the minimap, opens it: your portrait and who you are; the chapters on the left (a skull marks a close call, a star a rare); the chapter on the right. A second tab holds the Hall of the Fallen. When a level ends, a line in chat links to its chapter.

## Two packages

Each game has its own file: pick the one for yours (the CurseForge app does it for you).

- **Classic**: Classic Era, Hardcore, Season of Discovery, TBC Anniversary.
- **Forever**: World of Warcraft: Forever. Forever closes the combat log to addons, so a kill is counted when you target the corpse after fighting it.

## Settings

Options → AddOns → Wayfarer's Journal (or `/wj settings`, or right-click the minimap button): a line in chat for each chapter, the alert when a book closes, the minimap button. Where the game can't tell whether a character is Hardcore, a setting lets you say so.

Other commands: `/wj hall` opens the Hall of the Fallen; `/wj minimap` shows or hides the button.
