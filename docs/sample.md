## Chapter 1 (levels 1 to 4)

This is where it begins: Anvilmar, with nothing to my name but what I'm wearing. I gave Sten Stoutarm what they asked for: "Dwarven Outfitters". New foes in Coldridge Valley: wolves. A Rockjaw Trogg made the mistake of standing between me and the road there. "A New Threat", done, and a pint owed to me for it. My second level. I felt the Light draw nearer. I saw "Coldridge Valley Mail Delivery" through. Nobody died, least of all me. My third level. I'd drink to it if there were an ale in reach. A Frostmane Troll Whelp fell to my hammer. A Frostmane Troll Whelp had me at 9%. On this realm that is one bad roll from the end. I finished "The Troll Cave". Another small good in the world. Felix Whindlebolt asked for help with "The Stolen Journal". A paladin doesn't say no. My fourth level. I'll take it, and I'll take care. I came away from training with Blessing of Might and Judgement.

Five tasks, and every one of them a chance to die. I took none of those chances. Fourteen Frostmane Troll Whelps and twelve Burly Rockjaw Troggs fell to my hammer. An hour and twenty minutes, and three silver for the purse. A dwarf counts both. I slept well in Anvilmar, for once. Enough for one chapter.

## Chapter 2 (levels 4 to 6)

Anvilmar, by lamplight. I found Coldridge Pass. I stopped at Kharanos to look around, and stayed longer than I meant to. Thunderbrew Distillery has a bed I could get used to. I came back with what "Beer Basted Boar Ribs" wanted, and Ragnar Thunderbrew was glad of it. I came face to face with boars there, and learned fast. Something settled in me. My fifth level. Talin Keeneye had work for me: "The Boar Hunter". Done. My map grew by one name: Brewnall Village. I brought down a Leper Gnome there. I put my name to "Bitter Rivals", and kept my word. I slept rough in Shimmer Ridge, one hand on my hammer.

I woke in Shimmer Ridge to birdsong and a stiff neck. I put down a Frostmane Snowstrider there. Timber. I won't pretend it was easy; I'll only say it was me who walked away. I felt it: stronger, surer. My sixth level. Cuirboulle Gloves. Better gear, longer life. I wrapped up "Frostmane Hold". I did what Rejold Barleybrew asked of me: "The Perfect Stout". "Protecting the Herd", for Rudra Amberstill. They didn't ask how; I didn't tell. Mining at fifty. A trade is a life too, and safer than the other kind.

I worked: six jobs in all, the best of them "Protecting the Herd". Thirteen Frostmane Snowstriders and eleven Leper Gnomes, and never more than one at a time if I could help it. Two hours and a half of hard road. I rested in Thunderbrew Distillery, alive, with a door I could bar. That's the whole of happiness out here.

## Chapter 3 (levels 6 to 7)

I counted my blessings in Kharanos: all my fingers, all my toes, one life. I came to Loch Modan with my eyes open and my hand near my weapon. I spent an hour learning the paths of Thelsamar. I watched over Brannor, as a paladin should. I had to kill a Mountain Boar there. A fire, and the smell of something cooking. I finished "Thelsamar Blood Sausages" and took my reward. Another level: my seventh. I can feel the difference.
