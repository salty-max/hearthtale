# Sample: a Hardcore dwarf hunter, the first chapters

Generated by `luajit addon/test/sample.lua`: a life played through the addon in the test's fake game,
the events as the game sends them, written by the addon's own writer. A chapter runs from rest to rest
(the third is still being written).

## Chapter 1 (levels 1 to 4)

I begin in Coldridge Valley, with clean boots and no idea what I'm doing. I followed the road to Anvilmar, fought wolves for the first time and collected eight Tough Wolf Meat. I turned back for Coldridge Valley, brought down a Burly Rockjaw Trogg and hunted six Rockjaw Troggs, as Balir Frosthammer had asked. I went from Talin Keeneye to Grelin Whitebeard, bearing news. A Frostmane Troll Whelp had me at 9%. On this realm that is one bad roll from the end. I killed fourteen Frostmane Troll Whelps for Grelin Whitebeard, tracked down Grelin Whitebeard's Journal, then fitted myself with a Frostmane Leather Belt. I went back to Anvilmar, paid my trainer for Serpent Sting and Track Beasts, then passed on Durnan Furcutter's word to Marryk Nurribit.

Six tasks, and every one of them a chance to die. I took none of those chances. I fought until the Frostmane Troll Whelps and the Ragged Young Wolves ran short: seventeen and ten. All told: an hour and thirty-five minutes. I found a bed in Anvilmar before dark and slept like a stone. So ends this chapter.

## Chapter 2 (levels 4 to 7)

Another start, in Anvilmar. I wandered into Coldridge Pass. I reached Kharanos, took a room at Thunderbrew Distillery, then took up skinning. I began to learn leatherworking, learned what boars are like and found the Crag Boar Ribs Ragnar Thunderbrew wanted, six in all. After that, I put on a Handstitched Leather Vest, made with my own hands.

I saw the Grizzled Den with my own eyes and brought Hegnar Rumbleshot what Loslor Rudge had sent. I came to Brewnall Village for the first time, fought off a Leper Gnome and scoured the area for eight Gyromechanic Gears. Before long, I learned new tricks for the hunt: Arcane Shot. Later that day, I walked into Shimmer Ridge and dealt with a Frostmane Snowstrider. I kept watch there more than I slept.

Dawn in Shimmer Ridge, and every bone complaining. Onward. Timber fell to me there. I kept a trophy, and I keep thinking about it. I practised my skinning up to fifty and turned up a Frostmane Scepter among the spoils. I found my way to Frostmane Hold, cut down a Frostmane Headhunter, then did as Senir Whitebeard asked: explore the Frostmane Hold. I made it as far as Amberstill Ranch, then killed Vagash for Rudra Amberstill. I found myself back in Kharanos, then picked up Hunter's Mark and Raptor Strike at my trainer's.

Five tasks, start to finish. Eleven Leper Gnomes and ten Frostmane Snowstriders, and never more than one at a time if I could help it. Two hours and a half for this one. In Kharanos, a bed with sheets. This chapter ends here.

## Chapter 3 (levels 7 to 9)

I woke in Kharanos to a clear sky. I took the road to the Grizzled Den, had to kill a Young Wendigo and searched until I had eight Wendigo Manes. I went on with Thorgrim at my side. I followed the road to Gol'Bolar Quarry, then hunted down ten Rockjaw Bonesnappers. I headed back to Kharanos, then spent good coin on Concussive Shot and Mend Pet.

I walked on to Amberstill Ranch, then did the work: tame a Large Crag Boar. I tamed Bristle, a boar of the wilds. A fire in Amberstill Ranch. I sat with my back to it and my eyes on the dark. I finished off a Frostmane Seer. Bristle fell. Out here, it could as easily have been me.

I came to Loch Modan knowing the graves there are no shallower than anywhere else. I wandered into North Gate Pass. I reached Thelsamar, then put down a Mountain Boar.
