Generated by `luajit addon/test/writer.lua --sample` from a life as the game would record it: a chapter

## Chapter 1 (levels 1 to 4)

I mean to write everything down, so that someone will know I was here. It begins in Anvilmar. I brought Sten Stoutarm the Tough Wolf Meat they needed: eight of them. New foes in Coldridge Valley: wolves. A Rockjaw Trogg crossed my path there, and didn't cross another. Balir Frosthammer wanted six Rockjaw Troggs dead. I saw to it. Talin Keeneye had something for Grelin Whitebeard; I was the one to bring it. Then I dealt with a Frostmane Troll Whelp. A Frostmane Troll Whelp had me at 9%. On this realm that is one bad roll from the end. Fourteen Frostmane Troll Whelps, for Grelin Whitebeard. Soon after, I recovered Grelin Whitebeard's Journal and brought it back to Grelin Whitebeard. The trainer taught me Blessing of Might and Judgement.

Five tasks, and every one of them a chance to die. I took none of those chances. Fourteen Frostmane Troll Whelps and twelve Burly Rockjaw Troggs; I kept my hammer busy. That was an hour and twenty minutes. The day ended in Anvilmar, by a hearth and a full plate.

## Chapter 2 (levels 4 to 6)

Anvilmar, by lamplight. I found Coldridge Pass. Next, I stopped at Kharanos to look around, and stayed longer than I meant to. Thunderbrew Distillery has a bed I could get used to. I gathered six Crag Boar Ribs. That night, I came face to face with boars there, and learned fast. I hunted twelve Small Crag Boars, as Talin Keeneye had asked. After that, my map grew by one name: Brewnall Village. A Leper Gnome came out of the dark there. It didn't go back into it. I went from Rejold Barleybrew to Marleth Barleybrew, bearing news. I slept rough in Shimmer Ridge, one hand on my hammer.

I woke in Shimmer Ridge to birdsong and a stiff neck. I put down a Frostmane Snowstrider there. Timber. I won't pretend it was easy; I'll only say it was me who walked away. Cuirboulle Gloves. Good craftsmanship; I checked. After that, I did the work: explore the Frostmane Hold. I filled my bags with Shimmerweed, six in all. I found Vagash and made an end of it. I reached fifty in mining.

Six people helped, one way or another. Thirteen Frostmane Snowstriders and eleven Leper Gnomes, and never more than one at a time if I could help it. Two hours and a half of hard road. I rested in Thunderbrew Distillery, alive, with a door I could bar. That's the whole of happiness out here.

## Chapter 3 (levels 6 to 7)

The day found me in Kharanos. I left the familiar behind and came to Loch Modan. Then I spent an hour learning the paths of Thelsamar. I grouped with Brannor. More eyes, fewer graves. A Mountain Boar made the mistake of standing between me and the road there. I kept a fire burning through the dark hours. Vidra Hearthstove needed Bear Meat. I brought back three.
