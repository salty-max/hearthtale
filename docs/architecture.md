# Hearthtale's architecture (the addon)

The addon file by file (moved from CLAUDE.md on 20 October 2026; the map
stays there). The writing's files and rules: `docs/writing.md`. The site:
`docs/site.md`. The plan and its decisions: `PLAN.md`.

## The files

- Languages, the interface's (the journal is English on every client):
  every word the book, the Hall, the settings, the welcome and the chat
  lines show goes through `L["..."]` (the kit's `K.L`; plurals through
  `K.plural`), each sentence whole; `ns.upper` capitalizes accented letters
  too. The TOCs load the kit first, then `Locale.lua` and
  `Text_[TextLocale].lua`: the client loads its own. `scripts/locales.ts`
  (`bun run locales`, its `--check` in `addon:check`) writes them for the
  game's twelve languages from `i18n/<locale>/` (`words.json`, `meta.json`:
  plural rule, flagged); `--keys` writes `i18n/words.json`. The writer's
  files (Language, Lines, Diary, Forever, Names, Knowledge) have no `L`.
  `addon/test/french.lua`, `german.lua`: a French and a German client. French done 14 October 2026, German (flagged: a first translation) the same day.
  Spanish added 16 October 2026, flagged until native review: neutral tú,
  diario, entrada and Salón de los Caídos. esMX borrows esES prose while
  keeping its own race and building names. addon/test/spanish.lua runs on
  both Spanish clients and games, following the German scope: interface
  only, with the English writer and saved diary prose preserved. Individual
  death labels must not take the current reader's gender.
- The opt-in that shares what the game tells of the world (the kit's
  `K.collect`: quests' texts, creatures' facts, NPCs' lines, loot by source;
  off until the player ticks it on the welcome page or the Options page)
  keeps it account-wide in `HearthtaleCollect`, which Ravenpost sends to the
  addons' database.
- `addon/Hearthtale/Names.lua` (generated, committed): how the game itself
  writes its names, counted over all its quest texts and NPC speech: places
  with or without "the", creatures by name ("Hogger"), as one of a kind ("the
  Defias Messenger") or with "a", quest items' plurals ("8 Tough Wolf Meat"),
  role owners in item names ("a Champion's Helm" vs "Zanzil's Seal"). Where the
  game is silent, `scripts/names.ts` decides from the name (English words
  and given names from npm: an-array-of-english-words, human-names) and the
  writer's rules the rest. It also lists the places the why lines name
  (`writing/why/`): after writing one that names a new place, run the audit
  and commit Names.lua with it (the daily job opens a pull request
  otherwise; `bun run check` doesn't see it). `bun run audit`
  reads the data from wow-data's Classic Era database (every creature,
  quest, item, object, place and trainer's lesson; the game's own writing:
  quest texts and what creatures say) into `.cache/audit/`,
  regenerates Names.lua and runs `addon/test/audit.lua`: every place,
  creature and item of the game through the writer, reviewed in
  `.cache/audit/report.txt` and checked for regressions; then
  `addon/test/playthrough.lua`: each race played from level 1 to 30 on the
  game's real quests (its levelling road, real givers, targets, droppers,
  enders and rewards; a warlock's demons, a druid's forms, a hunter's pet and
  a shaman's totem quests at the original game's levels; a life's trades:
  taken up, their ranks, what they made, one given up for another or taken
  up again), every entry through
  the shared checks (`addon/test/inspect.lua`), the journals in
  `.cache/audit/books/` to read. Fix by rule, never by name.
- `addon/Hearthtale/Knowledge.lua` (generated by `scripts/knowledge.ts`, part
  of `bun run audit`, committed): what the game knows and the record doesn't,
  from the same database: a class's own quests, the spell each
  teaches and a shaman's element (`quests[id]`: its totem), and the quest
  chains (`chains[id]`: the first quest of its chain, from one-to-one links;
  `ends[id]`: the last one with work). The quest givers by name, their people
  (their model's race and sex, the client's own CreatureDisplayInfo) and
  calling, go to `.cache/audit/npcs.lua`, for the playthrough alone. Since 14
  October 2026 every script reads the databases (`openDb`): no dump, no CSV;
  a field the audit needs and the database lacks goes into wow-data's schema.
- `addon/Hearthtale/Client_Classic.lua` and `Client_Forever.lua` (generated by
  `scripts/client.ts`, committed; each game's own file): the client's own
  names in English, from its tables in wow-data's databases (the pinned builds): a
  trade's, a riding's, a talent tree's, a creature type's and family's and a
  flight master's name in every language; every area, dungeon, boss and
  class or trade spell by id; Forever's specializations named after the
  class; by id, English, the trades and ridings, the creature types and
  families, the flight points, the talent trees, the places inside buildings
  (`i18n/enUS/subzones.json`, the kit's) and the mounts' auras, for the saved
  file's ids (`Ids.lua`). `English_Classic.lua` and `English_Forever.lua` (the same script,
  from the audit's data and the client's item table): every creature, quest
  (its title, the creatures and items it asks for) and item in English by
  id, in a function a client in another language calls once (an English
  client never builds them); Forever's also names, behind its creatures and
  quests (`__index`), what wow-data's build leaves out of it (Season of
  Discovery's `left_out`): a saved file's id is never bare, and `Ids.lua`'s
  reverse lookups take its name only where the game's own lack it (Elaine
  Compton is Forever's 256391, a count by Polymorphed Apprentice 202093).
  Both are written by `bun run audit` and
  checked by `bun scripts/client.ts --check`, from wow-data's pins.
- The game's data comes through ~/code/wow-addons/wow-data
  (salty-max/wow-data, beside this folder): the clients' builds and the
  community databases pinned in its `pins.json`, each file downloaded once
  into its `.cache/`; the scripts import `../../wow-data/src/db` (`openDb`:
  the databases, the only thing they read). Hearthtale pins nothing of its
  own. `.github/workflows/game-data.yml` (daily and by hand) calls
  wow-data's `addon-data.yml` (wow-data beside it, the databases built from
  it downloaded), runs `bun run audit` and, when a
  generated file changed, opens a pull request with the journals compared
  before and after (`scripts/golden.sh`) and `bun run addon:check`, then
  rebased onto main by itself and told to the owner's phone in words
  (Ravenpost's inbox; GITHUB_TOKEN's pull requests and merges start no other
  workflow, CI included: the checks run in the job). wow-data's own daily job moves the pins when the game's
  builds move. It needs the repository setting "Allow GitHub Actions to
  create and approve pull requests" (Settings, Actions, General).
- `addon/Hearthtale/Forever.lua` (generated by `scripts/forever-data.ts`,
  part of `bun run audit`, committed; loaded by Forever's TOC alone,
  `Hearthtale_Camelot.toc`): what
  Knowledge.lua and Names.lua know, for Forever's own quests, creatures and
  places (Zephras Isle, the new quests in the old lands, the new dungeons).
  From wow-data's Forever database (the client's tables, Questie's Forever,
  its own quests' rewards and the prerequisites Questie lacks, AllTheThings',
  frozen there), new being what Classic Era's database doesn't know (as
  Knowledge.lua's); a quest's prerequisite one of Questie's alternatives,
  Forever's own first; names by `names.ts`'s rules over Classic's texts and
  Forever's together, only for names Classic lacks. It also writes
  `.cache/audit/game-forever.lua` and `npcs-forever.lua` for `FOREVER=1
  luajit addon/test/playthrough.lua` (the Skyborne of each side from Zephras
  Isle and the old races' new classes; journals in
  `.cache/audit/books-forever/`). No source has the new zones' quests above
  the beta's levels, nor any new creature's type or family. A quest
  the game titles "Call of Earth/Fire/Water/Air" is a shaman's own; a rite
  with no step known to give its totem gives it at its last step that
  brings the prize home ("Bring the Torch of Eternal Flame to …").
- `addon/Hearthtale/`: `Util.lua` (plain-Lua helpers: the deep copy),
  `Core.lua` (the character's record, events: `ns.on`, and `ns.onUnit` for a
  unit event heard for one unit only; `/hearthtale`, the kit's command,
  `K.commands`: alone it opens the journal, an unknown word prints the usage,
  the replies capitalised; the player's own words,
  `notes[n] = { title, text }`: a title given an entry, a note in its margin,
  plain text, from the window or `/ht title [N] TEXT`, `/ht note [N] TEXT`),
  the recorder in four
  files sharing `ns.record`: `Record.lua` (the record's schema, the chapters
  and their moments, the logouts, places, levels; a logout settled at the
  next login: under 30 minutes away it's no break, nothing told; resting and
  indoors as the game told them last while playing (Forever's tells neither at
  the logout itself), and a game that never tells: the hearthstone's own place
  a rest; at a login or a
  /reload, a line in chat (`ns.onResume`, Book.lua): "starting entry N" when
  the login began one, "resuming entry N" otherwise, "this life's book is
  closed" for a fallen one (said by Core.lua: a closed book hears nothing),
  with how to open the journal, as the Codex's; indoors
  without an inn, a night `inside`; a stop by a fire, one of Forever's camps
  or not, and the party at it), `Quests.lua`, `Combat.lua` (the
  creatures met, kills, close calls, death and the way back. Kills: the
  killing blow of mine, my pet's or my group's, from `PARTY_KILL` (killer,
  victim), an event of
  its own on Forever and Classic since 1.15.9 (secret only in a Forever
  instance, where no creature can be told), else the combat log's line; the
  name from what was seen (target, mouse-over, nameplates) or
  `UnitTokenFromGUID`, else from a creature of the same kind seen; and a
  quest's count gone up for a creature no kill told: another's blow on one I
  tagged, which the game credits me with) and `Life.lua` (company, dungeons,
  learning (a lesson's first cast before its chapter closes, kept on its
  moment: `used`), trades (taken up, a rank, given up when the whole skills list
  lacks it, taken up again), gear, loot, pets (tamed: soon after a Tame Beast; a new
  one otherwise came from the stable, noted quietly), a warlock's first demon
  of each kind and a druid's first form (learned in the journal), the first
  bag, the first gold piece, the first ride (riding known at the journal's
  first look: ridden before), money, a specialization: Forever's chosen one,
  else Classic's talent tree holding most of the points, ten at least, never
  the talents themselves). The highest level is a moment (`level` `top`);
  the journal goes on after it. A game function is
  checked before use only where the clients differ (a `C_` namespace, a
  function a client lacks); the test game (`addon/test/game.lua`) has every
  one the addon calls,
  the writer (the journal, written from the records when read), in three
  files sharing `ns.writer` (each exports at its end what the next ones
  import): `Language.lua` (pure helpers: numbers and lists in words, places,
  plurals, articles, items, what a foe is), `Lines.lua` (the Book: a book's
  choice of lines, `Book:say`; the scenery, the lessons, the prologue and the
  epitaph) and `Diary.lua` (`ns.writeBook`: { prologue, chapters = { {
  number, text: its entry, title, place, from, to, open, rare, close } },
  epitaph }, each chapter told as its entry: "The journal" below; the
  finished entries are kept per record with the book's memory after the last
  of them, every chapter but the last being finished, so a reading resumes
  there; the writer test checks a book written in pieces is the book written
  at once),
  `Save.lua` (the book written into the saved file at each logout, each
  chapter's entry in `diary`, its title (the player's when given one, the
  writer's then in `writtenTitle`) and the player's `note`, for the site: it
  never writes its own; a closed entry, the prologue and the epitaph keep the
  words saved there, `savedEntry` in Diary.lua: same begun and ended moments,

  its saved words),
  `Kit.lua` (a copy of the kit shared with the Codex, Yonder and

  the kit, `bun run kit:check` to verify), `Book.lua` (Hearthtale's ember
  theme on the kit; the window, the kit's book (`K.book`): the portrait, a
  Hardcore life's mark beside it (a skull and the word, Fallen once closed;
  on hover what it means and whether the game or the setting says so), the
  search (an entry found by its title, its place or its words), chapters on

  its header (the life's crest, a skull on a fallen one's; one line under
  the title: the entry and how it stands, where, which levels, when, a short
  date range; never wrapped, cut at its end), the player's note in its
  margin (the kit's aside), an Edit button at its end for its own title and
  note; a second tab for the Hall of the Fallen, the same list and page,

  `Hall.lua` (a Hardcore death: the book closed and copied to the

## The record in English, the saved file in ids

- English game clients only (a decision, 8 October 2026): the journal is
  written in English. The record keeps the game's names in English on any
  client (14 October 2026, after a life played in French then English told
  "mining and minage" and a path "Guerrier"): places, flights, trades,
  ridings, talent trees, spells, creature types and families, dungeons and
  bosses, creatures and quest givers (by the id in their GUID), quests (their
  titles and objectives, by the quest's id) and items (by the id in their
  link), by the game's ids or its own names in every language
  (`Client_<game>.lua`, `English_<game>.lua`, `scripts/client.ts`;
  `ns.record`'s `place`, `skill`, `spell`, `creature`, `link`…; a place
  inside a building by the language's list, `i18n/<locale>/subzones.json`,
  the kit's, in its Text file, `ns.gameNames`; a mount by its aura's id),
  and a record kept the old way is mended at login (`mend`, Record.lua). A
  name the tables lack (a quest's own words for an objective) still comes in
  the client's language. The saved file keeps ids, never a game name (the
  family's rule, 15 October 2026): at the logout, once the book is written,
  `Ids.lua` turns each name into its id (`ns.toIds`: a place "a<id>", inside
  a building "w<id>", a dungeon "m<id>", a creature's kind "t<id>"/"f<id>",
  a creature's or a quest's name false beside its id, an objective's thing
  by its id, a link without its name; trades, families, trees, spells,
  flight points, bosses, mounts by their numbers), only where the id gives
  the name back exactly, and the login turns them back (`ns.toNames`), this
  character's record and the Hall's lives: the recorders and the writer
  read the names, as before. A test's logout keeps the saved file's copy
  (`G.saved()`) and goes on as the next login would. `addon/test/languages.lua` plays a life in English,
  French and English again. Forever's specializations are one a class, named
  after it (no way chosen: the talents tell it).

## Lua tools

- Lua: `bun run format:lua` (StyLua, `stylua.toml`) and `bun run lint:lua`
  (selene, `selene.toml`; the game's globals in `wow.yml`: add one there when
  the addon calls a new game function). Both run in `addon:check` and CI.
